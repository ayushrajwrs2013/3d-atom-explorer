What is this 3D atom model?

An atom is the basic building block of matter. Every chemical element is made of atoms with a nucleus of protons (red) and neutrons (grey), surrounded by electrons (colored dots) that orbit in distinct electron shells (K, L, M, N, O, P, Q). This interactive tool lets you explore the Bohr model of any element from Hydrogen to Oganesson — click an element on the periodic table, then drag and zoom the 3D atom to inspect its electronic configuration and valency.
