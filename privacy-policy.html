Privacy Policy

3D Atom Explorer ("the App") — last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It is a purely educational visualization tool that runs entirely in your browser.

No accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no user tracking are used.

2. Third-Party Resources

The App loads the open-source Three.js JavaScript library from the Cloudflare CDN to render its 3D models. Cloudflare may log standard web requests (IP address, browser type) for security and CDN operation, subject to Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

3. Offline Storage

The App uses a service worker that caches page resources in your device's local storage so the App can work offline. This cache stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the app owner at: ayushrajwrs2013@gmail.com

5. Children's Privacy

The App is safe for children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
